Lam sach site data khi kho ngoai tuyen bi hong nang

Chi dung buoc nay khi man hinh Luu tru ngoai tuyen van bao loi sau khi ban da thu khoi phuc trong app. Viec xoa site data se don service worker, cache, IndexedDB va phien dang nhap tren trinh duyet nay.

Khi nao nen dung

  • - Da reset trong app nhung trang thai online-only van quay lai ngay.
  • - Chrome, Safari hoac Edge bao UnknownError hay backing store khi mo IndexedDB.
  • - QA can tra trinh duyet ve trang thai sach hoan toan de test lai PWA.

Cach lam an toan

  1. Dong tat ca tab dang mo cua holilihu.online tren trinh duyet nay.
  2. Vao phan Site settings hoac Clear browsing data cua browser.
  3. Xoa du lieu site cua holilihu.online.
  4. Mo lai LMS Maritime, dang nhap va tai lai cac khoa hoc ngoai tuyen neu can.

Luu y

Trang nay khong the tu dong xoa site data sau o moi trinh duyet. Neu ban chi can don cache PWA, hay quay lai man hinh khoi phuc trong app truoc.
