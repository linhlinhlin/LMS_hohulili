# Contributor licensing policy

New contributions to original project material are submitted under the MIT
License in `LICENSE`, unless the destination file or directory specifies
another license. Contributors retain their copyright; opening a pull request
does not assign it. Preserve existing copyright and license notices.

Contributions to `sdk/` must be authorized for distribution under Apache-2.0.
Third-party code must retain its original license and attribution and must not
be presented as an original contribution. Identify its source and license in
the contribution.

Contributors must have the right to submit the work, including employer
permission where applicable. Maintainers must not merge material when its
provenance or licensing authority is unclear.

The earlier AGPL/commercial contribution policy applied to earlier submissions.
Existing contributor agreements remain subject to their own terms. New MIT
contributions do not require a separate commercial relicensing agreement under
this policy; MIT itself includes permission to sublicense subject to its terms.

Meiiie is the project owner and licensor, operating under The Wiii Lab brand.
See `LICENSING.md` for the license map and `TRADEMARKS.md` for branding rules.
