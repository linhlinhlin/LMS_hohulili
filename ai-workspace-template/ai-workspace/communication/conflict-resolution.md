# Conflict Resolution

Protocol for resolving disagreements between agents.

---

## When Conflicts Occur

1. **Document** the conflict in shared-board.md
2. **Escalate** to PM if cannot resolve
3. **Escalate** to Human Owner for major decisions

---

## Resolution Steps

1. Both agents state their position clearly
2. PM reviews and makes initial decision
3. If significant, escalate to Human Owner
4. Document decision in decision-log.md

---

## Example

```markdown
## CONFLICT: [Short description]

Date: [DATE]
Between: [Agent 1] and [Agent 2]

### Position 1
[Agent 1's view]

### Position 2
[Agent 2's view]

### Resolution
[What was decided and why]
```
